# Validation

Run Verify_OMEGA.command on your Mac for the backend suite, ten UI template checks, and complete offline MLB report reproduction. The recovered formula is re-executed with outcomes and prices removed from its inputs. Chronological training, target-market independence, integer pushes, exact identity, failed collection, pregame timing and preserved NFL records are covered.

See audit/acceptance-tests.txt, audit/ui-template-checks.txt, and audit/mlb/reproduction-check.txt for the captured release checks. See MLB_BACKTEST_REPORT.md for historical evidence. Tests passing does not establish predictive advantage over a sportsbook or betting profitability.
